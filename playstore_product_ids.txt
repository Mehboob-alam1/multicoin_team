FIREBASE REALTIME DATABASE
--------------------------
Import config/firebase_iap_catalog.json at the database root (creates /iapCatalog).

Each Google Play purchase is stored under:
  users/{uid}/purchases/googleHistory/{pushId}
  users/{uid}/purchases/oneTime/{productId}     (packs / upgrades)
  users/{uid}/purchases/farmRigs/{pushId}       (each rig buy)
  users/{uid}/purchases/subscription            (premium plan + expiry)
